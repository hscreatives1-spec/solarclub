    Questions, answered.

    How much does a solar system cost?

    It depends on your load, roof and backup needs. Send us your requirement through the form and we’ll reply with a quote.

    Do I need batteries?

    Not always. Grid-tie systems work without batteries. Add batteries if you want power during outages.

    What is the difference between grid-tie and hybrid?

    A grid-tie inverter works with the grid and has no backup. A hybrid inverter also manages batteries, so it keeps your home running during outages.

    Do you install the system too?

    Yes. We survey, supply, install and commission it, then support you after handover.
